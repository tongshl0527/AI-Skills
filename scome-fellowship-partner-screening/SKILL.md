# SCOME fellowship partner screening

## Primary criterion (standing rule)
Rank every candidate first on: **can they provide or unlock internship placements for medical / life-science students?**

- **Hosts** score on whether they can take students into placements (capacity, role type, timing, supervision).
- **Connectors** (e.g. cooperatives, regional agencies, investors who open doors) score on whether they can unlock placements at other organisations, not on hosting themselves.
- Unknown capacity stays UNKNOWN; never invent placements or interest.

## Secondary criteria (after the primary)
Educational value, mentorship, non-confidential project/challenge fit, geography (Groningen / Northern NL preferred), existing relationship, board-clearance and reputational risk, overlap with another owner (one owner per company).

## Labels and promises
- Label facts VERIFIED / INTERNAL PROPOSAL / AI SUGGESTION.
- Screening for placement capacity is **internal**. Do not promise placements, internship numbers, partnership terms, or a finished design externally without the user's own approval plus required IFMSA authorisation.
- Soft in-person curiosity questions about local talent or placement interest may be OK under current event guardrails; partnership asks are not.

## Design conflict
If older design docs say "educational exposure only / not internships", flag NEEDS-DECISION and still apply this primary screen until the user changes it.

## Output
For each candidate: primary score (high / med / low / unknown), secondary notes, host vs connector, recommended next step (hold / soft mention / after pilots / Phase 1–2), and owner.
