# Partner screening across departments

Score every candidate on **every department lens**, not just one. A candidate who is weak for one department can be strong for another. Never drop a lens because the request came from a single department.

## Lenses (score each HIGH / MEDIUM / LOW / NONE, with a one-sentence reason)
1. **Student placements (fellowship or internship programme)**: can they realistically host or unlock internship placements for medical and life-science students? This is the programme's core need.
   - Role: HOST (could take students itself), UNLOCKER (opens doors to several hosts, e.g. a cooperative, development agency, investor portfolio or incubator), INDIRECT (knows hosts but has no lever) or NONE.
   - Weigh how relevant the hosts are to the field (pharma, biotech, CRO, medtech, diagnostics and clinical research rank highest), scale, supervision capacity (UNKNOWN if not known), competing pipelines, and timing.
2. **Speakers and lectures**: could they speak, or find speakers, on the lecture programme's themes?
3. **Startup / venture**: funding and subsidies (regional, national, EU), IP advice, investors and proof-of-concept funds, incubators, regional partners, pilot or test hosts, and domain advice. Fit depends on the actual product type (e.g. software or education tool vs hardware or medical device) and its stage (pre-entity vs company).
4. **Personal research and career** (when relevant): grants, consortia, labs, employers, mentors.

## Method
1. Read the owning departments' current goals and guardrails before scoring.
2. Score all four lenses. Mark each judgement VERIFIED (with a source) or INFERRED.
3. Say who benefits: the user personally, the committee or programme, or the startup.
4. Rank by the strongest single lens, then by breadth across lenses. Show the per-lens scores so each department sees its own view.
5. For each candidate add: timing (now / later / after pilots or entity), risks (two routes to the same partner, competing programmes, close networks where an early pitch travels, wording that creates expectations), and today's contact level (card only / soft mention / generic learning question / follow-up later).
6. Respect each department's wording and confidentiality rules. Internal scoring may use words that external conversation must avoid. Never contact, ask or commit on the user's behalf. Recommend only.
7. Align: send the draft to each department manager whose lens scores MEDIUM or higher. Each manager owns its own column. Report the aligned version to whoever asked.
