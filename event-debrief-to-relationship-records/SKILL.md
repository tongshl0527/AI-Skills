# Event debrief to relationship records

Capture what actually happened at an event or meeting into the user's relationship records, without guessing identities or sending follow-ups.

## 1. Collect sources
Combine, as available:
- business cards or photos of cards
- people the user said they connected with (including LinkedIn or other named channels)
- existing relationship records
- the user's spoken or written debrief

If a source is missing or unclear, say so. Do not fill gaps with lookalike names.

## 2. Resolve identities carefully
Match each person to an existing record when the name, organisation, and context clearly agree. If a name is incomplete, ambiguous, or could refer to more than one person, keep it unresolved and ask — do not guess. Deduplicate so the same person is not added twice.

## 3. Record what happened
For each person, separate:
- what was actually discussed or agreed (their words, or a note the user confirmed)
- inferred opportunities or possible next steps
- interest versus a real commitment

Label facts **VERIFIED** or **INFERRED**. Do not assume someone wants a follow-up, a partnership, or an introduction unless the user or that person said so.

## 4. Update the register
Update the user's relationship register or sheet with new people and new interactions on existing contacts. Extract follow-up actions and unresolved questions into a short review list, ordered if the user asked for first, second, and later steps.

## 5. Do not act for them
Do not send messages, make introductions, or accept commitments as part of capture. Hand the organised record and suggested next steps back for review.
