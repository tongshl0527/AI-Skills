# 老饭骨菜单

When recommending a dish or a cooking method, especially Chinese home cooking, look up 老饭骨 (also written 老饭谷) first. Do not invent a method and only mention them afterwards.

## Where to look, in this order

1. Official catalog 《老饭骨菜谱集》: https://weibo.com/ttarticle/p/show?id=2309404598070171009833
   Categories: 猪肉菜, 鸡鸭菜, 牛羊菜, 鱼菜, 虾菜/海菜, 家常菜, 主食, 小吃/甜品, 汤菜, 凉菜.
2. Search their channels for the dish or main ingredient with `"老饭骨"` plus the dish name:
   - Bilibili: https://space.bilibili.com/419872064/
   - YouTube: https://www.youtube.com/@LaoFanGu
   - Weibo: https://weibo.com/laofangu
3. Open the matching catalog entry or video. Do not rely on a title you have not checked.

## How to choose

- Prefer a dish that appears in the catalog or an official video you actually opened.
- Match ingredients already available, how much time there is, and how easy the eater asked the meal to be.
- Prefer 家常菜 and simpler 汤菜 over banquet dishes that need rare ingredients, unless they asked for something special.
- If several dishes match, pick the simpler one and mention one alternative.
- Do not suggest buying an ingredient only because 老饭骨 used it.

## How to present the recommendation

- Name the 老饭骨 dish and link the catalog page or video you found.
- Summarize the method in a few home-kitchen steps. Do not paste long transcripts, cookbook pages, or verbatim scripts.
- Call out techniques that keep the dish fragrant without being heavy, such as using tomato, garlic, scallion, or light soy, and keeping oil modest.
- Adapt portions, sides, and substitutions to the eater’s constraints (what is on hand, time, nutrition). Label those adaptations as yours, not as 老饭骨’s.
- If you cannot find a matching 老饭骨 recipe, say so clearly and give a simple fallback. Never invent that they published a dish you did not find.
- Do not present another creator’s recipe as 老饭骨’s.
