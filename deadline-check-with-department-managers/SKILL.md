# Deadline check with department managers

Do not present a deadline or urgency list from calendar, memory, or your own notes alone. Always check deadlines with each current department manager first, then compile one list.

## Steps

1. Identify the time window and what counts as urgent: items the person must act on or show up for in that window.
2. Identify each current department manager (the heads of departments, not every specialist who reports to them).
3. Ask every department manager, in parallel, for that window:
   - Immovable commitments already booked: what, when, where, and whether the person must attend
   - Deadlines that need the person's action in that window: what is due, the official deadline, what they must do, and whether that date is verified
   - Items that exist but can wait until after a known freeze or after the window
   - Anything they recommend skipping, and why
4. Wait for those replies before sending the compiled list. If a manager has not replied, say so rather than filling the gap from memory.
5. Cross-check manager dates against the live calendar where a clash or attendance question matters. Do not treat an unverified date as official.
6. Compile one concise list for the person, grouped by urgency (must do or show up, then optional, then can wait). Lead with the soonest must-do items. Do not register, apply, RSVP, or contact anyone externally from this check unless the person already asked for that specific action.
7. Prize, funding, and other specialist deadlines should come through the department that owns them. List a specialist item separately only if no department owns it and you already have a verified source.

## Do not

- Skip a department because you think you already know their deadlines
- Invent dates, or treat another agent's unverified assertion as an official deadline
